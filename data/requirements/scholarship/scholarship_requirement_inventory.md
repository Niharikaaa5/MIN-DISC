### Hierarchy Resolution Rules:
1. **Statutory Primacy:** Where statutory entitlements, reservation percentages, scholarship amounts, or broad eligibility rules are concerned, DOC-1 (`CSSS_GUIDLINES`) is the governing authority.
2. **Operational Detail:** Where DOC-1 is silent on specific implementation mechanics (e.g. OTR procedures, exact lists of uploaded files, drop-year rules), DOC-2 (`FAQ_DOHE_CSSS`) holds direct administrative authority for AY 2025–26.
3. **Temporal Scope (AY 2025–26 Boundary):** DOC-2 explicitly governs **Academic Year 2025–26**. Scheme administrators issue periodic operational updates. Operational restrictions specific to DOC-2 (such as the drop-year disqualification) cannot be generalized to AY 2026–27 or future academic years without confirmation from the official DoHE notification for that specific cycle.
4. **Legacy Training Material:** DOC-3 (`training_of_users_nsp`) reflects historical NSP UI flows (AY 2020–21). Generic portal features (such as manual bank passbook uploads or bonafide certificate uploads) are superseded for CSSS by current scheme-specific guidelines (DOC-1) and operational FAQs (DOC-2).

### 2.2 Family Income Criterion vs. Document Submission Rule

*Crucial Distinction: The substantive income ceiling is distinct from the procedural document submission rule.*

1. **Substantive Income Criterion:**
   * **Rule:** Gross parental/family annual income **must not exceed ₹4.5 Lakh per annum** (≤ ₹4,50,000 p.a.) (DOC-1: p. 2, Sec 4.v; DOC-2: p. 1, Q1).
   * **Quote:** *"Students with gross parental/family income upto ₹ 4.5 lakh per annum are eligible for scholarship under the scheme."* (DOC-1, p. 2)
   * **Scope of the Condition:** The ₹4.5 lakh limit is a foundational eligibility condition of the scheme. **The supplied sources do NOT establish that renewal applicants are exempt from the underlying ₹4.5 lakh income threshold.** It cannot be assumed that a renewal applicant whose family income exceeds ₹4.5 lakh remains legally eligible.
2. **Procedural Document Submission Rule:**
   * **Rule:** An Income Certificate document is **required to be submitted/uploaded ONLY for Fresh applicants** (DOC-1: p. 2, Sec 4.v; DOC-2: p. 6, Q19.a).
   * **Quote:** *"Income certificate will be required only for the fresh applicants;"* (DOC-1, p. 2)
   * **Renewal Documentation Status:** Renewal applicants are exempt from uploading an income certificate to the portal (DOC-2, p. 6, Q19.b). This is an administrative documentation waiver for renewals, not an established waiver of the substantive ₹4.5L income criterion.

An applicant in an inactive AISHE institution may complete application registration, but disbursal is blocked until the institution updates its status.

### 3.2 Conditional College-Transfer Verification Category (State Nodal Agency Procedure)

*College ID cards are NOT standard portal uploads under Q19. They belong to a distinct administrative transfer procedure.*

* **Trigger Condition:** Student changing their college/institution during the 2nd or 3rd year of the course.
* **Administrative Channel:** Student must contact their **State Nodal Agency (SNA)** at least 2 weeks prior to the closing date of NSP (DOC-2, p. 8).
* **Documents Required for Transfer:**
  1. Previous and Present College ID card (DOC-2, p. 8).
  2. Present and Previous College Name and AISHE Code (DOC-2, p. 8).
  3. Present and Previous Course details (DOC-2, p. 8).
* **Verbatim Quotation (DOC-2, p. 8):**
  > *"For change in college during 2nd and 3rd year of the course, kindly contact your State Nodal Agency at least 2 weeks prior to closing date of NSP along with following documents: (a) Previous and Present College ID card, (b) Present and Previous College Name and AISHE Code, (c) Present and Previous Course. The requests for change in college received in the last 7 days prior to closing of NSP will not be entertained."*
* **Documentary Status:** These are conditional artifacts submitted in support of an administrative institution change request to the State Nodal Agency. The source does **not** establish that College ID cards are a mandatory upload on the standard NSP scholarship application form.

### 4.2 Inferred Attributes Required for Eligibility Evaluation and Quota Allocation

These attributes represent business logic variables, derived metrics, or institutional verification assertions necessary to assess eligibility or allocate quota slots, but are **not** explicitly enumerated as applicant form text inputs in the sources:

1. **`class_12_percentile` / `merit_list_status`:** Inferred evaluation metric. Derived by comparing the applicant's Class XII score against the Board-specific Top 20th percentile cutoff list supplied directly by State Boards (DOC-2, p. 3 Q10.a).
2. **`gap_year_flag` / `drop_year_status`:** Inferred validation check. Evaluated by comparing the Class XII passing year with the admission year to enforce the AY 2025–26 restriction against drop years (DOC-2, p. 1 Q1).
3. **`other_scholarships_flag`:** Inferred compliance check. An applicant declaration that they are not availing any other scholarship or fee reimbursement (DOC-1, p. 2 Sec 4.iv).
4. **`attendance_percentage` ($\ge 75\%$):** Institutional verification metric. Certified by the Institute Nodal Officer (INO) during the institutional verification phase for renewals (DOC-1, p. 2 Sec 4.viii).
5. **`annual_examination_marks` ($\ge 50\%$):** Renewal academic criterion. Extracted from the uploaded previous year marksheet and verified by INO (DOC-1, p. 2 Sec 4.viii; DOC-2, p. 6 Q19.b).
6. **`stream_ratio_quota` (3:3:1):** System-side quota allocation metric. Slots are distributed among Humanities, Science, and Commerce (DOC-1, p. 1 Sec 3); not an applicant-entered value.
7. **`gender_earmarking_quota` (50% girls):** System-side quota allocation rule (DOC-1, p. 1 Sec 3).

## 7. Unresolved Operational Questions & Technical Design Hypotheses

*This section explicitly distinguishes verified requirements established in the sources from open questions and proposed design approaches.*

### 7.1 Unresolved Operational Questions (Source Gaps)

1. **Board Percentile Cutoff Source:**
   * *Source Fact:* DOC-1 (p. 1) mandates "above 80th percentile", while DOC-2 (p. 3, Q10.a) states State Boards supply a definitive Top 20th percentile list directly to NSP.
   * *Unresolved Question:* Does the portal validate raw marks entered by the student, or does it perform an automated database lookup against the Board's uploaded merit list?
2. **Authority Rank for Income Certificate:**
   * *Source Fact:* DOC-1 (p. 2) and DOC-2 (p. 6) require a "Family Income Certificate".
   * *Unresolved Question:* The documents do not specify designated state revenue ranks (e.g. Tehsildar, Revenue Officer, Sub-Divisional Magistrate). The exact acceptable issuing authority rank varies by State.
3. **Transmission Medium for College Transfer ID Cards:**
   * *Source Fact:* DOC-2 (p. 8) states students changing colleges must contact the State Nodal Agency $\ge 2$ weeks prior along with College ID cards.
